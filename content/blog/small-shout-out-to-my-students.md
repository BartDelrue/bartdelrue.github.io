This semester, a group of students in the **web topics advanced** elective explored *linked data*,
*federated web*, *ActivityPub*, *Webmention*, and *Solid*. They also experimented with tools such as *three.js*,
*cables.gl*, and *TouchDesigner* to create interactive media projects.

LinkedIn-style boasting isn't my strong suit, but I'm very proud of the work they've done. It was inspiring to see
how they approached these topics with curiosity and determination, finding their own ways to make sense of complex
concepts and turn them into something tangible.

I invited them to Courtray for the [Wonder creativity festival](https://wonderkortrijk.be/), took them to the
[KBR](https://www.kbr.be/) in Brussels to meet the wonderful [Sven Lieber](https://sven-lieber.org) for an
inspirational talk about linked data, and half of them *still* hadn't had enough and voluntarily joined me at
[Wintercircus Ghent](https://www.wintercircus.be) for a talk about TouchDesigner!

To [Jelle Michiels](https://jellemichiels.vercel.app/), [Jarno Willems](https://jarnowillems.vercel.app/),
[Wout Desmedt](https://woutdesmedt.vercel.app/), Seth Van de Veire, Kwinten De Deyn, Arno Van Hee, and
[Brent Bauwens](https://2425wta-project-frontend.vercel.app/): thank you for making this semester an engaging and
enjoyable experience. I'm looking forward to seeing what you'll create next!

*P.S. – I'm using this post to test a **Webmention**. Let's see if it finds its way to you!*
